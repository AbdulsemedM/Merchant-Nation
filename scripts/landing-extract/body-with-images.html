The Operation

Four moves. One mission cycle.

The same cycle repeats across every branch, every day — simple enough to run from a phone in the field, structured enough to roll up into real reporting.

01 — RECON

Scout

Tap a cell on the live territory map and file a recon report — business name, category, daily volume, a photo. Thirty seconds in the field.

+20 XP
02 — ONBOARD

Induct

Convert a scouted lead into a registered merchant through a three-step wizard: verify, KYC & products, oath.

+100 XP
03 — DEPLOY

Execute

Accept missions and tasks from your branch manager, work them from Pending to Submitted to Approved.

Branch goals
04 — ADVANCE

Grow

Every action earns XP. Climb the rank ladder, build a streak, and see exactly where you stand on the leaderboard.
